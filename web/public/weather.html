Radar nowcast on live data

Every five minutes a scheduled job finds the strongest storm cells the NEXRAD radar network is tracking, gathers observations for each from NOAA, NWS and USGS, and runs an Aven model compiled to WebAssembly on them. Each forecast is scored against the radar once its time arrives.

Three ways to predict the radar echo at a site 15, 30 and 60 minutes ahead. Persistence assumes the echo stays as it is, the standard baseline; the other two carry the echo now upstream along the steering wind (NOAA winds aloft) or along the storm motion the radar network measured. A method shows skill only where it beats persistence. MAE is the mean absolute error in dBZ (lower is better); CSI is the critical success index for rain at 20 dBZ or more (higher is better).

How it works

The model is examples/weather_nowcast.aven: moisture (Ambaum 2020 saturation curve), hydrostatic density with virtual temperature, a plane fit through three stations' winds for divergence, vorticity and deformation, vertical shear, Marshall-Palmer rain rates, and the nowcasts. avenc emit-wasm compiles it to a 4 KB module, weather.wasm, which the job runs and which your browser can re-run from any stored inputs with the button on each run. Its exp and fractional powers come from the JavaScript host (aven_math.js).

verify/weather_reference.py checks the same model on 150 random atmospheric states against the relational version (evaluations/possible/weather-predict.aven, solved by avenc), MetPy, NumPy and the closed-form formulas; every output agrees within 1e-9 relative.

What the checks do and don't show

  • The data-quality gate is the bounds in weather-predict.aven; a run that breaks one is stored but issues no forecast.
  • Humidity and pressure compare two processing pipelines for the same station's sensors, so they catch decoding and reduction errors, not a faulty sensor. Radar against a rain gauge, and steering wind against radar-tracked storm motion, compare independent measurements.
  • Scores are for single points in the base reflectivity mosaic. Storm-cell sites are chosen where echoes are strong, which favours forecasts of rain; the fixed airports are a control.
  • Lagrangian persistence is a baseline nowcast, not a weather model: it moves today's echoes and cannot grow or decay them.
